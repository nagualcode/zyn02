# Community banks

Instruments made by the ZynAddSubFX / Yoshimi community, bundled with Zyn02
and shown first in the browser under **COMMUNITY**. The patches are
unchanged apart from their display name; NeoSoundFonts patches also have
their (empty) author and comment fields filled in with the credit below.

Every patch was rendered with Zyn02 and with the original ZynAddSubFX 3.0.x
(same notes, same random seed). A patch was only included when the two
renders have the same level (within 1 dB RMS). About half of them are bit-for-bit
identical; the rest use Zyn's own randomness (PADsynth phases, random pan,
unison phases) and differ only in the way two runs of the original
differ from each other. Seven patches that did not meet this bar were left out.

## Classic Bass · Classic Leads & Synths · Classic Pads & Strings · Classic Bells & Voices

117 patches by **Yingchun Soul** (迎春心情) and the **NeoSoundFonts** project,
many of them re-creations of well-known sounds (TB-303 style acid bass,
super saw, Italo-disco bass and leads, 80s pads, synth brass and strings,
arranger-keyboard classics), taken from:

- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/Escape-from-Y-Arrangers
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/cc0-italo-disco-preset-pack
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/supersaw-preset-pack
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/Escape-from-Game-Sounds
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/Crisis-DX-source-files
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/Aspirin-DX-Soundbank
- https://github.com/NeoSoundFonts/LibreArachno-Source-Files

License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). Also published on
https://musical-artifacts.com.

## Yoshimi Chip · Yoshimi Companion II

From the banks distributed with Yoshimi (https://github.com/Yoshimi/yoshimi,
`banks/chip` and `banks/Will_Godfrey_Companion`):

- **Yoshimi Chip**: 49 chiptune patches by Christopher "Chip" (Airlynx) Van Dan.
- **Yoshimi Companion II**: 33 patches by Will J. Godfrey that are not in
  the Companion bank shipped with ZynAddSubFX (B3, Theatre and Cathedral
  organs, Accordion, Cello, Tomita Whistle, choirs and more).

License: **GPL-2.0-or-later** (as Yoshimi), distributed here under GPL-3.0.
